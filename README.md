# Fabrication and Performance Analysis of a 3D-Printed Double Arrowhead Auxetic Structure for Impact Mitigation

Undergraduate thesis, **Department of Mechanical and Production Engineering (MPE), Islamic University of Technology (IUT)**, September 2025.
Design, simulation, fabrication and experimental testing of a **Double Arrowhead (DAH) auxetic**
structure as impact-protective padding.

**Authors:** Maysha Anan Aishy, Naoshin Anzum Islam, Zahin Tasnim Ridita
**Supervisor:** Prof. Dr. Md. Zahid Hossain, Dept. of MPE, IUT

📄 **[Full thesis (PDF)](thesis/Fabrication_and_Performance_Analysis_of_3D-Printed_DAH_Auxetic_Structure.pdf)**

---

## Summary

Auxetic structures have a **negative Poisson's ratio** — they contract laterally under compression,
concentrating material under an impact and absorbing energy efficiently. This thesis develops a DAH
auxetic padding and compares it against solid padding for impact protection.

**Headline result:** the DAH TPU padding showed **significantly lower peak force transmission** than
solid TPU padding, together with a **67.4 % weight reduction** in the final design — better
protection for far less mass.

## Approach

- **CAD:** Double Arrowhead auxetic unit cells and lattices modelled parametrically in **SolidWorks**.
- **FEA:** non-linear **ANSYS** simulations of stress distribution, deformation and impact response
  under drop-impact loading.
- **Fabrication:** specimens **FDM 3D-printed in TPU**.
- **Testing:** experimental **drop-impact** and **tensile** tests used to validate the simulations.

*Keywords:* auxetic structure, double arrowhead, negative Poisson's ratio, impact response,
finite element analysis, additive manufacturing, TPU.
